FAQs

Common Questions

How long does it take to build my website?
Most Starter sites launch in 2–3 weeks. Growth projects typically take 4–6 weeks. Pro projects are scoped individually but usually 6–10 weeks. We'll give you a clear timeline upfront.
Do I need to provide my own content?
We'll guide you through exactly what content we need. For Growth and Pro plans, we also offer optional copywriting services to craft compelling, SEO-friendly content for you.
Can I make updates to my site after launch?
Yes! Growth and Pro sites include a CMS so you can edit text, images, and blog posts yourself without touching code. We also provide a training session so you feel confident.
What happens after the project is done?
You own 100% of your site. We offer ongoing maintenance plans starting at $99/month that cover security, updates, backups, and up to 2 hours of content changes per month.
Do you offer payment plans?
Yes. We require a 50% deposit to begin, with the remaining 50% due on launch day. For Pro projects, we can discuss a 3-payment structure. Just ask us!

Our Story

Built by People Who Love the Web

Webvio started with a simple frustration: too many great businesses had terrible websites. Outdated designs, slow load times, and zero strategy — all holding them back from the growth they deserved.

I'm Leslie Ughulu, founder of Webvio, and I created this company after seeing firsthand how many talented professionals were being overlooked simply because their online presence didn't match the quality of their work. Incredible brands were losing opportunities every single day — not because they weren't good, but because they weren't visible.

In 2026, visibility isn't optional — it's survival. No matter what you do — whether you're a lawyer, a music artist, a fitness coach, a contractor, or running a tech startup — your online presence is your first impression, your storefront, and your credibility all in one. People search before they call, browse before they buy, and judge your professionalism long before they ever meet you. If your brand isn't visible, clear, and compelling online, you're leaving growth on the table.

So we built a different kind of web agency. One that combines strategic thinking with exceptional craftsmanship, moves fast without cutting corners, and genuinely cares about the long-term success of every client we work with.

Today, Webvio is a team of professionals spread across three time zones, having launched 150+ websites for businesses across industries — from wellness and legal services to eCommerce and SaaS. Every single project we take on gets treated like it's our own, because your success is ultimately our reputation.
